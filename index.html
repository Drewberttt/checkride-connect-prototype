A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear answers,
before you start.

Can I book a real checkride here today?

Not yet. This is an interactive prototype with fictional examiners, students, fees, and openings. It demonstrates the proposed experience; there is no verified DPE directory and no real booking is made.

Is a request automatically confirmed?

No. Request-and-accept scheduling is central to Checkride Connect. A request stays pending until the examiner accepts it. In a future live service, fees, policies, and test details would need to be agreed before confirmation.

What can I try in the demo?

Switch between student, examiner, and flight-school previews. Browse sample profiles, send a request, accept it as an examiner, customize a checklist, and try the simulated conversation. Changes stay in your browser, and you can reset the demo. Please don’t enter sensitive or real student information.

Does the checklist confirm that I’m eligible?

No. A checklist helps coordinate preparation; it is not an official eligibility determination. Students, instructors, and examiners still need to confirm all applicable requirements. Preferred airports also do not establish an examiner’s authorization.

Is this affiliated with the FAA?

No. Checkride Connect is an independent concept, not an FAA service or endorsement. The prototype does not connect to FAA or IACRA systems.
